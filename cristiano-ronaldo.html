Biography

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro, born February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal, is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers and most prolific goalscorers in football history. Beginning his career at Sporting CP, Ronaldo moved to Manchester United at age 18, where he transformed into a world-class winger and striker under Sir Alex Ferguson. His transformation into a prolific goal-scorer is one of modern football's greatest success stories.

With 950+ career goals across all competitions, Ronaldo holds the record for most goals in professional football history. His career spans Manchester United (2003-2009), Real Madrid (2009-2018), Juventus (2018-2021), Manchester United again (2021-2022), and Al-Nassr (2023-present). A 5-time Ballon d'Or winner (2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017) and Portugal's all-time leading international goalscorer with 145 goals in 220+ caps, Ronaldo led Portugal to Euro 2020 victory. Continuing to perform at elite level at age 41, he remains one of football's greatest competitive forces.
