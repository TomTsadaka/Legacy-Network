Biography

Lionel Andrés Messi, born June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina, is universally recognized as one of the greatest footballers of all time. After joining Barcelona's La Cantera at age 13, he developed into a record-breaking goal-scorer and playmaker who defined an era of dominance. With 927 career goals and 460 assists across all competitions, Messi's legacy extends far beyond statistics—his technical brilliance, football intelligence, and humble demeanor revolutionized modern football.

Messi won eight Ballon d'Or awards, more than any other player in history. His career spanned Barcelona (2004-2021, 672 goals in 778 matches), Paris Saint-Germain (2021-2023), and Inter Miami CF (2023-present). The pinnacle came in 2022 when, at age 35, he led Argentina to World Cup victory in Qatar, fulfilling his lifelong dream. Now 38, playing for Inter Miami, Messi continues to perform at elite levels, winning the 2025 MLS Golden Boot with 29 goals and 19 assists.
