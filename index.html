Biography

Lionel Andrés Messi is an Argentine professional footballer who is widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. Born in Rosario, Argentina, Messi joined Barcelona's youth academy at age 13 and rose to prominence at Barcelona, winning numerous trophies including the FIFA World Cup with Argentina in 2022.

Known for his exceptional dribbling, ball control, and playmaking ability, Messi spent most of his career at Barcelona before moving to Paris Saint-Germain in 2021 and later to Inter Miami CF. His career achievements include multiple La Liga titles, UEFA Champions League wins, and an Olympic gold medal.
