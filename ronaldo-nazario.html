Biography

Ronaldo Luiz Nazário de Lima, born September 18, 1976, in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, is considered "The Phenomenon" - one of the greatest strikers in football history. His explosive combination of speed, strength, technical ability, and goal-scoring prowess revolutionized the role of the modern striker. Despite career-threatening knee injuries that cut short what could have been an even greater legacy, Ronaldo's peak years remain unmatched in terms of pure dominance.

Known for his incredible athletic gifts and lethal finishing, Ronaldo won the FIFA World Player of the Year award 3 times (1996, 1997, 2000) and led Brazil to World Cup victories in 1994 (as 18-year-old squad member) and 2002 (where he was the tournament's top scorer with 8 goals). Playing for PSV Eindhoven, Barcelona, Inter Milan, Real Madrid, and AC Milan, Ronaldo scored 414 club goals in 616 matches. His partnership with Zinedine Zidane and Luiz Figo at Real Madrid created one of the most dominant attacking forces in Champions League history.
