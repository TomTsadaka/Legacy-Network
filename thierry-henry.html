Biography

Thierry Donald Henry, born August 17, 1977, in Paris, France, is Arsenal's greatest ever player and one of the Premier League's most dominant strikers of all time. Beginning his career at AS Monaco before transferring to Arsenal in 1999 for £11 million, Henry transformed into an explosive, elegant, and lethal forward who defined an era of dominance at the North London club. His combination of pace, power, technical ability, and intelligence made him nearly impossible to defend against.

Wearing Arsenal's number 14 before switching to 12, Henry won the Premier League 4 times with Arsenal (2002, 2004, 2005, 2006) and FA Cup twice (2003, 2005). He scored a record 228 goals in 377 Arsenal appearances, establishing himself as the club's greatest goal-scorer. Henry also played for Barcelona, New York Red Bulls, and AS Monaco in his later career, representing France 51 times with 51 international goals. His elegant, intelligent style of play and devastating finishing established him as one of football's true modern greats.
