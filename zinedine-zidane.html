Biography

Zinedine Yazid Zidane, born June 23, 1972, in Marseille, France, is widely regarded as one of the most technically gifted midfielders and greatest players of all time. Known for his exceptional left foot, elegant ball control, and ability to decide the biggest matches, Zidane was the orchestrator of French football's greatest era, leading his nation to consecutive tournament victories and defining a generation of players.

Winner of the FIFA World Player of the Year award 3 times (1998, 2000, 2003), Zidane led France to World Cup victory in 1998 and European Championship in 2000. His career at Juventus and Real Madrid established him as one of football's most complete players - capable of scoring, creating, and leading from midfield. Famous for his iconic performances in Champions League finals and his decisive performances in international tournaments, Zidane scored 95 international goals across 108 caps and 309 club goals, establishing himself as one of football's true legends.
